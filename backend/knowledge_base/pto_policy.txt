Employee Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of PTO per calendar year, accrued monthly at 1.5 days.
Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year.
PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for approval by a manager.
New hires become eligible to use accrued PTO after completing a 90-day probationary period.
Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and provides 10 days per year.
Parental leave is 16 weeks paid, separate from PTO and sick leave balances.

Refund Policy (Effective 2026)
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase for any unused product.
Digital subscriptions are refundable within 14 days of the billing date.
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.
Shipping fees are non-refundable except in cases of a defective or incorrect item.
Enterprise contracts follow the refund terms specified in the signed order form and
are not covered by the standard 30-day policy.
Store credit is issued instead of a cash refund when the original payment method is
no longer valid.
